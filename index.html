    01 · About us

    Raahe is building India's first ecosystem for artists. A stage to show raw talent, and a way to get paid for it.

    We started Raahe because the maths never added up. India watches more live performance than almost anywhere on earth, and the people making it still struggle to pay rent. So we built the bit that was missing. Nights where new artists actually get a stage, clients who actually pay them, and a crowd that turns up for both.

    No follower count. No showreel. No contacts in the industry. If you can hold a room, you are in.

    Centre stage

    A real room, a real audience

    Our open mics give artists a proper stage, a proper sound system and a hundred people who came to listen. Not a phone camera in a bedroom.

    First paid gig

    The first paycheck for the art

    We connect artists to venues, brands and events that pay. For most of the people we scout, this is the first money their craft has ever made them.

    Open to everyone

    Talent is the only filter

    No follower count, no showreel, no contacts needed. If you can hold a room, you belong on the lineup.

    Portfolio

    Proof you can send anyone

    Every performance becomes part of an artist profile. Clips, credits and past gigs in one link, ready for the next booking.

    Community and collab

    The people you make things with

    A guitarist meets a poet meets a producer. Most of what our artists build next comes out of a room we put them in.

    03 · Raahe offline

    The open mic, run properly.

    A Raahe Open Mic is a community night where new artists get their first spotlight. Musicians, poets, comedians and storytellers, in front of a real audience. We put the whole evening together ourselves, from finding the lineup to bringing in small businesses, so it feels closer to a mini festival than a sign up sheet on a clipboard.

    04 · Selected artists

    the artists. program

    An invite only track for artists we think are ready to go further. You get mentorship, introductions that actually turn into work, and first call on the best paying gigs, collaborations and events we run. Musician, dancer, actor or comic, if the craft is there the program is built to take it professional.

    • Mentorship

      Regular sessions with working artists and industry people who have already done the thing you are trying to do.

    • Network

      Direct introductions to venues, labels, brands and organisers. No cold DMs.

    • Priority gigs

      First access to the best paid gigs, collabs and events on our roster.

    06 · The floor

    Two ways in

    For artists and audiences

    Join the community

    The community is the strongest thing we have built, and you do not have to perform to be in it. If you just like watching people be good at something, that counts. Members hear about every lineup, every gig call and every collab first.

    Join the community
    For venues and spaces

    Host an open mic

    Cafes, restaurants, pubs and creative spaces: we bring 100 to 150 people through your doors over a couple of hours, and they stay to eat and drink. We can co-host the night with you as a live event. All we need is the space and the tech to run it.
