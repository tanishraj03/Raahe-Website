HOW TO SAVE THEM
================
Open the post on a computer, click through to the slide you want, right click
the photo and choose Save image as. Rename it to match the list above.

Keep every file under about 400 KB. Squoosh.app compresses for free.
Anything missing just shows a coloured tile, so nothing breaks while you collect them.

Want a different number of photos? Add or delete the <figure class="shot"> lines
in index.html. Both halves of each list have to match or the loop will jump.
